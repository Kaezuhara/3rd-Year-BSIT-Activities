HealthFirst Diagnostic Clinic Network Infrastructure

  • Cisco Packet Tracer
  • Networking

An enterprise-grade network for a multi-branch healthcare organization, built in Cisco Packet Tracer. It connects a branch clinic in Quezon City to Headquarters for more than 150 users across every department, keeping patient data separate from guest traffic. It supports IP telephony, video teleconsultations, guest Wi-Fi, centralized records over the WAN, and high availability during link or device failures.

Group project, team lead. I led the team and designed the network structure, diagram, and topologies, plus the IP addressing plan.
